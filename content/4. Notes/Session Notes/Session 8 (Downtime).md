### The room itself
Stone walls, low ceiling, no windows, shelves along two walls, a table in the center covered in notes, vials, and other papers. The room is surrounded in books and other oddities On the shelves: 
* Six identical blue glass vials labeled in Wyhan's precise hand: "**Accelerant Batch 3** NOT stable above 80°F." These are the combustible substances Wyhan has been developing. Essentially portable fire bombs hurl as an action, 10 ft. radius, DC 13 Dex save or 3d6 fire damage, half on save. Three are stable. Two have small cracks in the glass. One is sweating. the vials make you uneasy especially the one that's sweating.
- A **handwritten beastiary**: not Wyhan's hand, older, in Draconic. Pages on sivak draconians specifically: their shapeshifting ability, the fact that they copy the last humanoid they kill, how long the form holds, the weakness that breaks it. Several passages are underlined
- Three spell scrolls: **Alter self** / **Detect Thoughts** / **Tongues**
* A map of kalaman: with patrol routes marked in red, river gate circled twice
