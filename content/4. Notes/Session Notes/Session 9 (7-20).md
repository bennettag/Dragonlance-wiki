#### Retaking Wheelwatch Outpost

[[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]] tasks the party with retaking Wheelwatch Outpost from the [[Red Dragon Army]]. Recon spots an armored dragon rider patrolling the skies above.

The party poses as mercenaries delivering prisoners to bluff their way through the gate, but the cover is blown almost immediately and a deadly battle erupts. The gates open just in time for reinforcements to pour in as [[Davran Raes'Vir]] turns the tide with a bolt of lightning. The dragon rider breaks off and flees as the fort falls.

Searching the prison, the party recovers two notes:
- **Recent dispatch** (carried by the fleeing rider): details Dragon Army troop movements — confirmation that more than just the Red wing is on the move.
- **Older battle plan**: a trap laid for the Kalaman army at Steelsprings — a small force staged as bait, followed by an ambush from special forces dragon riders led by **[[Red Ruin]]**. No date given.

Among the prisoners, the party frees **[[Elgo]]**, a self-proclaimed famous adventurer and explorer, locked up for insisting she be allowed to explore the fort.
