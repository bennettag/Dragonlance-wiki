#### On the Water
- A morose atmosphere hangs over the ships, the weight of loss still heavy on the villager's minds
- Lore: As a child, Darrit traveled across the realms of Ansalon and was abandoned by his parents in Vogler.
- Darrit and the party talk about the future as heroes, and potentially joining Knights of Solamnia or an order of high sorcery
- [[Kalaman]] comes into view, Mayor Raven asks us to join her as she intends to speak with the city's council

#### Arrival at Kalaman
- Citizens and workers of the docks are surprised to see our arrival
- Bakaris, Vessa, and son get off quickly, glance back at the ship and disappear into the city.
- [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] - towering mid 40s woman. Commander of [[Kalaman]]'s military
- Villagers are escorted to the Almhouse of Mishikal to be cared for.
- We are directed to the Hall of Lords to inform the council.

#### The Hall of Lords
- Large impressive room. Guards, assistants, and others stand at attention
- 6 Council members
	- [[Calof Miat]] 
	- [[Alexandra Nightvane]]
	- [[Uri Stonegrey]]
	- [[Oflyn Vinhollow]]
	- [[Renold Costwyn]]
	- [[Sigmund Pross]]
- During Mayor Raven's recounting of events, Uri's eyes are stirring, as if his mind is racing.
- Davran asks the council what they know about [[Kansaldi]]. During this time, the whole room is filled with chaotic whispers.
	- At Kansaldi's name being mentioned, Uri's eyes shift. Dimitri gets the sense that some information was just sent somewhere in the city.
- The council is overall not very receptive to the warnings of the mayor or the party.
	- Calof - happy to house, feed, and register town members, but doesn't want to overreact to "lizards"
	- Sigmund - eagerly suggests putting together a fighting force to eliminate the army
	- Renold - Sarcastically criticizes the council's non belief in the party's words, while still offering no support
	- Oflyn - Recommends doubling the harbor watch but not to take further action
	- Alexandra - Asks the party what they suggest
- At the end of the meeting, its clear the council members are all generally self-interested. It will be hard to convince them to help in a meaningful way
- As the scene ends
	- Uri watches us leave with a lingering gaze
	- Sigmund asks to send for Bakaris
- We talk afterwards about finding the "Benebog" of Kalaman to gain the council or even the general people's trust.
- Also suggest figuring out how to make council members invested in the cause.
