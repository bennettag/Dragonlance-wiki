#### Dockhouse

[[Asher Owyn]], [[Dimitri Forgehammer]], [[Gregor Fairmount]], and [[Davran Raes'Vir]] breach the dockhouse through the skylight, dropping in on the [[Fire Fishers]] — the dockworker crew under [[Oflyn Vinhollow]]'s command — mid-operation.

- **Boilerdrak components:** scattered pieces and materials appear to be parts of the Boilerdrak, all stamped with the [[Red Dragon Army|red dragon]] insignia
- The crates are bound for a warehouse in Tinker's Row — a second delivery point beyond the docks
- The workers claim ignorance: they don't know what's going on, they're "just working"

As we work the room, a cloaked figure outside catches our eye, then bolts. We give chase.

#### Chase in the Streets

The figure is acrobatic, vaulting rooftop to rooftop and proving hard to follow.

- The figure looses a messenger pigeon to the south; [[Davran Raes'Vir]] strikes it down before it can carry word
- [[Asher Owyn]] drops a *fog cloud*, but the figure's glasses flash blue and they navigate the murk unhindered
- [[Davran Raes'Vir]] attempts a heroic leap from a rooftop onto [[Gregor Fairmount]]'s horse — he takes a bad fall, barely managing to haul himself up onto the horse
- [[Asher Owyn]] strikes the figure down
- [[Gregor Fairmount]] and [[Davran Raes'Vir]] catch up on horseback

#### Interrogation at the Steady Beacon

The cloaked figure is **[[Greyfeather]]** himself — the courier. Brought back to the Steady Beacon and pressed for information, he talks:

- **The route:** a dead drop at the docks, then run it north to the masked rider where the Vingaard forks
	- The rider is unsettling, and never speaks
- He asks who we know:
	- *[[Alexandra Nightvane]]* — a flash of opportunity crosses his face
	- *[[Wyhan]]* — he thinks she's dangerous

In exchange for the intel, we agree to help him secure asylum with [[Alexandra Nightvane]]. [[Jesen Thold]] protests that [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] won't like this, but agrees to escort him afterward.

What Greyfeather gives up:
- **They plan to sack the city.** [[Oflyn Vinhollow]] is behind it all
- [[Wyhan]] may not know everything, but she is involved
- **39 Tinker's Row** — that's where we'll find Oflyn. But something is wrong with him; "he's not what he seems"
	- On a past drop, Greyfeather saw something dragging Oflyn's body down into a basement dwelling
	- He saw Oflyn alive the next day — and got an unmistakably creepy vibe
- He shows us a coded message: an **internal force** will help sack the city, supplied with guard patrol paths and other intelligence about Kalaman
- He insists no other council members are involved

As we prepare to leave, [[Jesen Thold]] remarks that someone [[Gregor Fairmount]] would really like to meet has been staying at the Beacon — but offers nothing further. We leave Greyfeather with Jesen and head for Tinker's Row.

#### Tinker's Row

We snoop around the warehouse and slip down into the basement.

- In a chest, we find **Oflyn's dead body.**

Above us, [[Wyhan]] and "Oflyn" are talking — we overhear:
- Oflyn asks whether Wyhan wants the city sacked
- He argues that being sacked is preferable to being razed
- Plans are already in motion, and he has told "powerful allies" about her
- He whispers some kind of proposal; Wyhan says she will consider it
- She'll have all the reagents ready by the time they're needed
- They discuss the activity at the docks — they know it was us, but are confident we know little, even about Tinker's Row
- Oflyn promises Wyhan a place working alongside the [[Red Dragon Army]]
- He mentions *"the red dragon army that looms beneath"*

"Oflyn" says he needs to check on the basement. As we attack, he transforms into a silver [[Draconians|draconian]].
