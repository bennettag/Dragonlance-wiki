#### Dinner with Nightvane

[[Alexandra Nightvane]]'s guard lowers over a lavish feast, a stark contrast to Vogler, as she reminisces about her mother and her own adventuring days mapping the world.

The party and Alexandra exchange information:
- **[[Oflyn Vinhollow]]:** 3 dockworkers filed complaints against him, all quit within a week; one left for the capitol
- **[[Davran Raes'Vir]]** demonstrates the Dragon Army's sigils via illusion
- **Wyhan:** Black Robe, former trainer/evaluator of adepts. Withdrawn since her last candidate, something happened involving a Nothic and a student. Alexandra pulls back when pressed on suspicion
- **Boilerdrak:** Party shares details on materials and mechanics. Alexandra notes the machine is old, and concludes the army's intent is conquest of cities, not raiding towns

**Alexandra's read on the council:**
- *[[Sigmund Pross]]* - conspiring with Bakaris; building something, and fast
- *[[Calof Miat]]* - clean but unreliable
- *[[Uri Stonegrey]]* - enigmatic; intent unclear
- *[[Renold Costwyn]]* - too distracted (devices, women) to lead; fears he is cursed by Zeboam (God of Seas), avoids water (father and grandfather both drowned)

*[[Amulet of Reorx]]* - appears in some estate records from a northern holding (low confidence lead)

Alexandra offers alliance. She has reach the Governor and [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] lack and only asks for periodic information sharing. She's particularly interested in Nestra's accounting, suspecting the Marshal withholds things from the council. When [[Davran Raes'Vir]] presses her on ulterior motives, she invokes family and lineage as her true investment.

[[Gregor Fairmount]] and Alexandra spar philosophically; she poses a trolley problem he dismisses as unrealistic. We share that her interests and Nestra's are broadly aligned. She provides a city map with key locations marked near the Docks and Wyhan's Apothecary.

**We agree to share information going forward.**

#### The Docks

[[Asher Owyn]] and [[Dimitri Forgehammer]] scout the circled building while [[Gregor Fairmount]] and [[Davran Raes'Vir]] hang back. Observations:
- Wheelbarrow matching one seen at Wyhan's
- Crane extended upward; lights on late at night; sound of water inside, suggesting an interior dock
- Smoke from a window, work ongoing

[[Asher Owyn]] intercepts a pigeon carrying a coded message, then eavesdrops on two voices (one foreign, one local):
> *"When the Highlord's column reaches the valley road, the gate schedule changes. Vinhollow knows what to do."*

He also swipes a stack of papers. Key finds:
- **Crate markings:** "Tinkers Row Consignment" with a stylized C; accompanied by a reagent list
- **Intercepted note:** *"The eastern patrol window is confirmed, second bell to fourth, River Gate, three guards not four as previously reported. The offering stands. When the standard is raised we will be ready. V. trusts nothing. Keep the channels clean. Burn this."*
- **Coded pigeon message:** *"Masked rider to the north. Greyfeather's pigeon went missing. O is concerned, are we still set for the plan?"*

**Flags pointing toward action:**
- Wheelbarrow ties this location to Wyhan's Apothecary
- Foreign voice openly discussing betrayal of Kalaman's gate schedules
- A figure spotted carrying an oil-soaked wrapped item, possibly incendiary
- Sailors on watch, visibly hostile to observers
- Greyfeather's pigeons route through here; workers who complained about him went missing
- Late-night lights and active operations, something is being prepared
- Multiple entry points (crane, water, windows, doors)
