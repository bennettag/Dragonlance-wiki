#### Evening at Wheelwatch

[[Tatina Rookledust|Rookledust]] catches the party up: Kalaman forces have been dispatched to engage the [[Red Dragon Army]], against [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s wishes. Vendri was away, and [[Vessa Drayne and family#Bakaris|Bakaris]] seized the opportunity to take command, marching troops to Steelsprings, 30 miles west of the city.

[[Darrit]] rouses the remaining troops to set out and meet the Kalaman forces at Steelsprings, leaving a handful of men behind to hold the fort. As the party rides for Steelsprings, Darrit muses it might not be the worst idea to "get a dragon" of their own — whether that's even possible remains to be seen.

#### Fight at Steelsprings

The party runs into a Dragon Abomination. **[[Red Ruin]]** makes a drive-by pass overhead, loosing an explosive crossbow bolt.

The party barely escapes with a handful of soldiers in tow. [[Vessa Drayne and family#Bakaris|Bakaris]], shaken, reveals he's lost his son in the fighting. Even so, word starts to spread — [[Darrit]]'s band of soldiers is earning a reputation for what they pulled off.

#### Return to Kalaman

Back in the city, [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]] is furious with Bakaris for marching men to their deaths against her orders.

[[Gregor Fairmount]] is knighted, becoming a Knight of the Crown.

Word arrives that Solamnic Knights of the Rose have shown up unannounced at the War Room. The party rushes in on the sound of screams to find the council slaughtered — Sigmund Pross, Calof Miat, Uri Stonegrey, and Renold Costwyn all dead. Only [[Alexandra Nightvane]] survives. A man in Solamnic colors is waiting for them: **[[Karradoc]]**, who tells them the Knights of Solamnia are en route before he's swiftly cut down. A spectral, possessing spirit tears free of his body and flies downward — presumably toward **[[Lord Soth]]**.
