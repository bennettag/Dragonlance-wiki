**Weapon Mastery Options** available

**Exhaustion:** Each level of exhaustion applies a -2 penalty to all rolls and a 5ft reduction to speed. At level 6, you die.

**Surprise**: If a combatant is surprised by combat starting, that combatant has Disadvantage on their Initiative roll.
**Initiative Swapping**: If two players share consecutive initiatives, they can choose who goes first.

**Potions**: Bonus Action to use.

**Short Rests**: Requires 10 minutes of resting. You regain 1/2 your max hp (and any class resources). Max 2 per long rest.

**Inspiration:** Earned through roleplay rather than starting the session with it.
