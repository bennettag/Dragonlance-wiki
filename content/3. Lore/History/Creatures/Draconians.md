Draconians are spawned by warping and mutating metallic dragon eggs. Legends say chromatic dragons would steal chromatic eggs and turn them into these creatures.

- We fought some during the ambush outside of [[Vogler]] in [[Session 0.5 (4-06)]]. Upon death, they turned to stone and released a paralyzing gas.
