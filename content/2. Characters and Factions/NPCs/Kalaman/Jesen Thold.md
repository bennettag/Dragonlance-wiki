### Background

Barkeep of the Steady Beacon in [[Kalaman]] and a trusted confidant of [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] — she routes her private messages through him, and there are hints of affection between them.

### In-Story Interactions

- As barkeep of the Steady Beacon, recognized [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] and, at her request, set up a private table in the back; his fingers brushed hers for a moment, hinting at affection. Nestra instructed the party to send word to her only through Jesen ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- Protested the party's deal to grant the courier [[Greyfeather]] asylum, warning that [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] wouldn't approve, but agreed to escort him afterward. Mentioned that someone [[Gregor Fairmount]] would really like to meet had been staying at the Beacon, without saying more ([[Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
