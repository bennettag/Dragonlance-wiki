### Background

Wyhan is a Black Robe of the high mage society, operating out of Wyhan's Apothecary in [[Kalaman]]. She is a former trainer and evaluator of adepts, testing candidates for worthiness. She has been withdrawn since her last candidate, following an unspoken incident involving a Nothic and a student.

### In-Story Interactions

- [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] flagged Wyhan's Apothecary as suspicious, noting it had been purchasing supplies beyond what it would reasonably need. [[Greyfeather]]'s pigeons were also observed traveling to Wyhan, and a note intercepted by Rin hinted at Wyhan's involvement in redirecting a courier's sealed orders ("W sends their regards") ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- The party visited the apothecary. Asher scouted the exterior and noted several delivery crates, pigeons coming and going from the roof, abjuration magic warding the outside, and a burly tattooed dockworker picking up a packet and returning to the docks. Inside, Wyhan greeted the party. She spoke privately into Davran's mind: "Something in you is waking up, is it not? Or perhaps coming back?" and "Your vision, the one you ran from. It wasn't a vision of something that might happen. It was a memory of what will come to pass." She gave the party a potion of healing and a potion of fire resistance, and said only that she was "doing what she should be doing." Gregor noticed a monocle of smoked glass with a rim matching Wyhan's necklace, and a Tidewater Lens she described as waiting for a "tide" (350g) ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- Alexandra Nightvane confirmed Wyhan is a Black Robe and former adept evaluator, but pulled back when asked directly whether she suspected her ([[Session 7 (6-09)|Session 7]])
- A wheelbarrow matching one seen outside Wyhan's Apothecary was spotted at the suspicious dockside building the party investigated, suggesting a supply connection ([[Session 7 (6-09)|Session 7]])
- At a warehouse on Tinker's Row, the party overheard Wyhan in conversation with "Oflyn" (in truth a [[Draconians|draconian]] impostor). He pressed her on whether she wanted the city sacked — arguing it was preferable to being razed — told her he had spoken of her to "powerful allies," and offered her a place working alongside the [[Red Dragon Army]]. Wyhan agreed to consider his whispered proposal and to have all the necessary reagents ready when needed ([[Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
