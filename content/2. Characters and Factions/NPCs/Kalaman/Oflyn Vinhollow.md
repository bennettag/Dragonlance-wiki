### Background

Head of the Dockhand's Guild in [[Kalaman]] and a member of the city council.

### In-Story Interactions

- At the Hall of Lords council meeting, recommended doubling the harbor watch but opposed taking any further action ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- The party raided the dockhouse and caught Oflyn's [[Fire Fishers]] crating [[Red Dragon Army|red dragon]]-marked Boilerdrak components for delivery to a warehouse at Tinker's Row. Under interrogation, the courier [[Greyfeather]] named Oflyn as the architect of a plot to sack the city, and warned that something was wrong with him — he had once seen Oflyn's body dragged down into a basement, only to see Oflyn alive the next day ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
- At 39 Tinker's Row the party found Oflyn's true corpse hidden in a chest. The "Oflyn" plotting with [[Wyhan]] above them was an impostor; when the party attacked, it transformed into a silver [[Draconians|draconian]]. A scroll on its body read, "[[Lord Soth]] is ready" ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
