### Background

Head of the Cartographer's Guild in [[Kalaman]] and a member of the city council. She appears to carry the most weight in council meetings at the Hall of Lords.

### In-Story Interactions

- At the Hall of Lords council meeting, she was the one who asked the party directly what they suggested, showing more genuine engagement than her peers ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- Invited the party to dinner after the council meeting to give a more personal account of the events in [[Vogler]] ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- Hosted the party for dinner at her estate. Her guard lowered over the meal as she reminisced about her mother and her adventuring days. Proposed a mutual information-sharing arrangement, offering her read on each council member and a city map with key locations marked. Expressed particular interest in [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]]'s plans and accounting. When pressed on her motives, cited family and lineage as her true investment ([[Session 7 (6-09)|Session 7]])
- In exchange for intelligence, the party agreed to help broker asylum for the courier [[Greyfeather]] with Nightvane, despite [[Jesen Thold]]'s caution that [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] would object ([[Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
