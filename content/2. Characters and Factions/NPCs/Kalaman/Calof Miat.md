### Background

Governor of [[Kalaman]] and a member of the city council. Though he holds the formal position of Governor, his voice doesn't carry much weight on the council, at least in the shadow of [[Alexandra Nightvane]].

### In-Story Interactions

- At the Hall of Lords council meeting, he was willing to house, feed, and register the [[Vogler]] refugees, but dismissed the threat as overreacting to "lizards" and pushed back against taking further action ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- Killed along with the rest of the council when Kalaman's War Room was slaughtered ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
