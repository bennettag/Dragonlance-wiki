### Background

Commander of [[Kalaman]]'s military. A towering woman in her mid-forties.

### In-Story Interactions

- Met the party at the docks upon their arrival with the [[Vogler]] refugees ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- Invited the party to meet with her at Castle Kalaman ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
- Hosted the party at the Steady Beacon and laid out the threat to [[Kalaman]]: the city is unprepared for the Dragon Army, a council vote may be swinging (aided by Bakaris), and 3 of her last 4 patrols were ambushed despite only council and staff knowing the routes — pointing to spies. She flagged [[Wyhan]]'s Apothecary, the [[Fire Fishers]] (dockworkers under [[Oflyn Vinhollow]]), and the courier [[Greyfeather]] as leads, and tasked the party with finding who is feeding the Dragon Army — identified, not killed — reporting only through [[Jesen Thold]]. She noted she is wary of [[Alexandra Nightvane]], neither friend nor foe ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- When the party brokered asylum for the courier Greyfeather with [[Alexandra Nightvane]], [[Jesen Thold]] warned that Nestra would not approve of the arrangement ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
- Furious with [[Vessa Drayne and family#Bakaris|Bakaris]] after he seized command of Kalaman's forces in her absence and marched men to their deaths at Steelsprings against her orders ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
