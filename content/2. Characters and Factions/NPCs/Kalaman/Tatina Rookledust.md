### Background

A gnome inventor recruited from the Dargaard Mountains south of [[Kalaman]]. Unwittingly built the device that was reverse-engineered into the dracolich machine that tore through [[Vogler]] — a fact she carries with grim resolve rather than denial. Talkative, resourceful, and quick to turn a bad accident into a useful invention.

### In-Story Interactions

- Rescued by the party from a goblin siege on her workshop, having refused to hand over a second commissioned device to [[Kansaldi]]'s forces ([[Session 8.9 (Out of Session)|Session 8.9]])
- Gifted the party a pair of **fargabs** — long-range communication devices she built by accident while trying to make a weapon ([[Session 8.9 (Out of Session)|Session 8.9]])
- Joined the party and [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s war council at Kalaman, sketching calculations beneath the table while the room planned around her ([[Session 8.9 (Out of Session)|Session 8.9]])
- Relayed word at Wheelwatch that Kalaman forces had been dispatched to engage the [[Red Dragon Army]] against [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s wishes, and that [[Vessa Drayne and family#Bakaris|Bakaris]] had seized command in her absence ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
