### Background

A grizzled Kalaman military officer under [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s command. Arrives like weather — blood-marked, weary, and carrying a stillness that puts a room on notice.

### In-Story Interactions

- Burst into the war room to report Wheelwatch Outpost's fall to the [[Red Dragon Army]], and asked Mayor Raven, who played in the fort as a child, to brief the party on its layout ahead of retaking it ([[Session 8.9 (Out of Session)|Session 8.9]])
- Led the retaking of Wheelwatch Outpost alongside the party ([[Session 9 (7-20)|Session 9]])
- Rouses the remaining troops at Wheelwatch to set out and meet the Kalaman forces at Steelsprings after [[Vessa Drayne and family#Bakaris|Bakaris]] seized command in [[Marshal Nestra Vendri|Vendri]]'s absence. Muses it might not be a bad idea to "get a dragon" of their own ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
- His band of soldiers survives the fight at Steelsprings and starts earning a reputation for it ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
