### Background

A reclusive courier operating in [[Kalaman]], known simply as "the courier." He runs messages by carrier pigeon — his birds travel to [[Oflyn Vinhollow]] and [[Wyhan]] — and keeps to himself, with a reputation for eating his own pigeons. He works an unofficial dead-drop route, carrying packages from the docks north to a silent masked rider where the Vingaard forks.

### In-Story Interactions

- Named by [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] as "the courier," his pigeons traveling to [[Oflyn Vinhollow]] and [[Wyhan]]. Rin at the Hall of Records had heard strange things about him: he keeps to himself and eats his own pigeons ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- An intercepted coded message noted that "Greyfeather's pigeon went missing"; his pigeons routed through the dockside building the party investigated, and workers who had complained about him had gone missing ([[Session 7 (6-09)|Session 7]])
- Revealed to be the cloaked figure the party chased down and captured. Under interrogation at the Steady Beacon he described his dead-drop route — docks to the masked rider at the Vingaard fork — and gave up the plot to sack the city: [[Oflyn Vinhollow]] is behind it, [[Wyhan]] is involved, Oflyn can be found at 39 Tinker's Row, and an internal force armed with guard patrol paths will aid the attack. He insisted no council members were involved. In exchange he sought asylum with [[Alexandra Nightvane]], and was left in [[Jesen Thold]]'s keeping ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
