### Background

**Lord Soth — the Knight of the Black Rose.** Neither living nor dead: the Fallen Rose, the Death Knight.

Once a Knight of the Rose, the highest and proudest of the order, Soth fell further than any knight in Krynn's history. Warned that he could stop the [[The Cataclysm|Cataclysm]] itself if he turned from his sins, he chose pride instead. The gods cursed him not with death but with undeath — a lord wrapped in cold flame, bound to the ruin of his own keep, the spectral elf-women who damned him whispering the night of his fall to him for eternity.

He is not a soldier to be fought but a storm to be survived: the dead rise where he walks, brave men's hearts stop at his approach, and the orange fire of his eyes is the last warm thing many a hero ever sees. He does not boast, hurry, or forgive — least of all himself.

A power like Soth bows to no Dragon Highlord and serves no army's chain of command. If he has stirred and turned his attention north, it is not at any Highlord's order, but because a war has begun grand and damned enough to be worth his while — and only one commander on this front wages a war like that.

> *"I was offered the salvation of the world, and I chose my pride. I do not follow queens. But I have, on occasion, found one worth watching."*
