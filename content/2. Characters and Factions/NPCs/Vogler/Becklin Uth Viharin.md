Becklin Uth Viharin (BEK-lin ooth ve-HAR-in) - Human Knight of Solamnia stationed in [[Vogler]]

### Background

A Knight of Solamnia posted at Thornwall Keep in [[Vogler]].

### Pre-Story Connections

- Known to the whole party through her presence in town
- [[Gregor Fairmount]] and [[Asher Owyn]] trained with and under her

### In-Story Interactions

- Wrote a note to the party before [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s funeral ([[Prelude]])
- At [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s reception at the Brass Crab with [[Darit]]; sat with [[Gregor Fairmount|Gregor]] and [[Asher Owyn|Asher]]. When asked about prospects as knights and how the party could help, she was clear: do not act alone, get help if needed, and do nothing to cause a panic during a time of celebration ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- Along with Cudgel, lowered [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s body into the water at the reception ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- Presented the party with a box [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]] had left for them, containing his green shield ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- Asked the party to participate in the Battle of High Hill reenactment ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- The party reported their initial [[Draconians|draconian]] encounter to her ([[Session 0.5 (4-06)|Session 0.5]])
- At the emergency town meeting after the betrayal at High Hill, she declared that the War of the Lance may be upon them again ([[Session 2 (4-20)|Session 2]])
- Coordinated the town's defense and evacuation plan, including using the town bell as the evacuation signal ([[Session 2 (4-20)|Session 2]])
- Left a gift in her quarters for [[Darit]] ([[Session 2 (4-20)|Session 2]])
- Was taken hostage on the docks during the fall of [[Vogler]], then executed by [[Kansaldi]] as the party watched from the water ([[Session 4 (5-05)|Session 4]])
