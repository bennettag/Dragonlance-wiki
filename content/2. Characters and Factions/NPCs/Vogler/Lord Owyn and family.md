A prominent merchant family in [[Vogler]]. The Owyn name carries old weight in town, but the family's commercial reach expanded dramatically after Mira arrived. Lord Owyn expects his sons to pursue knighthood; Mira ensures the business runs.

---

### Lord Owyn

A former soldier and merchant lord. He came up through the military and has never quite let go of the expectation that his sons will follow the knight's path. He cares for [[Asher Owyn]] genuinely but struggles to feel pride in a son whose gifts don't look like the ones he was raised to value.

**Pre-Story Connections**
- Father of [[Asher Owyn]]
- Raised Asher alongside two older brothers, expecting all three to pursue knighthood

**In-Story Interactions**
- The Owyn family was present at [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s reception at the Brass Crab ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])

---

### Mira

The driving force behind the Owyn family's commercial success. She arrived in [[Vogler]] with Lord Owyn after a period of his grief-driven travel and quietly took the reins of everything the family touched without ever raising her voice or making a scene. She never married Lord Owyn. She has earned genuine respect in town through her steady presence and business acumen, though the circumstances of her relationship with Lord Owyn have never been fully forgiven by Vogler's more proper residents. An old friend of hers, the wizard [[Lewin]], visited throughout [[Asher Owyn|Asher]]'s childhood. His recent unexplained silence worries her, though she has not yet shown panic.

**Pre-Story Connections**
- Mother of [[Asher Owyn]]
- Taught Asher to read a room, to negotiate, and to be the most prepared person at the table without letting anyone know it
