### Background

Retired captain. Weathered and spry, with deep pockets he has no interest in spending. He fishes the [[Vogler]] waterfront most days and keeps mostly to himself. He deals in cryptic wisdom the way other men deal in coin. He once told [[Gregor Fairmount]] that "the fish that fights the current longest is the one that never reaches the sea," and on another occasion, unprompted: "a sword knows nothing of the hand that swings it, but the hand knows everything." There is a steadying warmth to him that doesn't announce itself. Gregor suspects he looks out for him in ways he's never made explicit.

### Pre-Story Connections

- Mentor figure and friend to [[Gregor Fairmount]], whose friendship began at the wharf during Gregor's hauling days. Gregor was always the one who stopped to ask about a strange catch or an unfamiliar fish, which seemed to earn a quiet fondness. Gregor began bringing him a spiced dark drink from the local tavern whenever his boat came in. Theron never asked for it but always accepted warmly, and once it was in hand he would talk. He has also promised Gregor a ride on his ship one day, offhandedly, the way people sometimes do. Gregor has not forgotten.
