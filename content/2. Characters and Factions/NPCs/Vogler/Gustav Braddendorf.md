### Background

An old human very late in his years. A cobbler by trade, now retired and being cared for by his eldest daughter Hilda. He was the first human [[Davran Raes'Vir]] allowed himself to become truly connected to. Seeing him near the end of his life brings Davran a bittersweet appreciation for everything their long friendship has been.

### Pre-Story Connections

- Lifelong friend of [[Davran Raes'Vir]], close since Gustav was in his twenties, sharing irresponsible drinking, double dates, his wedding, the births of his children, and even grandchildren
