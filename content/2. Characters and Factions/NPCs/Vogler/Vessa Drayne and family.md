A family of landed nobility in [[Vogler]]. Well-connected and self-interested, they carry themselves with a snobbish air and have shown a consistent willingness to prioritize their own comfort over the community around them.

---

### Vessa Drayne

Landowner and town council member. Sharp-tongued and well-connected, with little fondness for the Solamnic order. She operates through rumor, social pressure, and bureaucratic obstruction. Nothing she does is technically dishonorable, which makes her all the more difficult to confront.

She once fed [[Gregor Fairmount]] a convincing fabricated story about a local shopkeeper, complete with documentation, witness names, and dates. Gregor hauled the man out of his shop in front of the entire market, hands bound. By the time the charges were dropped, the shop had been ransacked and cleaned out. The shape of it pointed clearly to Vessa having used Gregor as a distraction, but nothing could be proved. Gregor apologized to the shopkeeper. It wasn't enough.

She once put up fliers warning parents about the dangers of magical items, specifically targeting [[Davran Raes'Vir]]'s business after she received a shock from one of his wares. She and Davran exchange cutting "compliments" about each other's outfits whenever forced to interact.
