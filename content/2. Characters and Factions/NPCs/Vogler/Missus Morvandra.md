### Background

A matronly Kender woman who runs the orphanage in [[Vogler]]. Loving but strict, she tries to impart on those in her care that kindness is free to give but should not be expected or relied on to survive. She is a reliable source of town gossip, as the orphans have a way of overhearing more than people realize.

### Pre-Story Connections

- [[Davran Raes'Vir]] volunteers at the orphanage regularly, reading to the children, teaching simple skills, and donating food or coin in good months. The two enjoy a deep mutual gossip partnership.
- [[Gregor Fairmount]] runs informal sparring sessions for children in town, including some from the orphanage. The sessions are more play than real training, but real training swords have been involved and some children have come away with more bruises than Morvandra approved of. She has been cast in an unflattering satirical role in the children's games (Morvandra the Destroyer, Morvandra the Undying, Morvandra the Demon Queen), which she takes with mixed feelings.
