### Background

A human adventurer and master storyteller, now deceased. He was a beloved and familiar presence in [[Vogler]], known for the tales he told.

### Pre-Story Connections

- [[Gregor Fairmount]]: Ispin gave him his first book and opened the world to dragons, heroes, and the grandeur of adventuring
- [[Davran Raes'Vir]]: Ispin reminded him of the wanderlust of his younger years
- [[Asher Owyn]]: Asher used Ispin's stories to understand the world, working to dilute the fables down to their facts
- [[Dimitri Forgehammer]]: Was always drawn to Ispin's stories of the gods

### In-Story Interactions

- The party was invited to his funeral in [[Vogler]], with word that he had something he wished to tell them ([[Prelude]])
- At the reception at the Brass Crab, [[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]] and Cudgel lowered his body into the water ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- The party recounted stories at the reception and each gained legendary inspiration ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- [[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]] shared a story Ispin loved about Mt. Nevermind, a gnomish engineer named Fizz, and retrieving a launched weasel ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- Ispin left a box for the party containing his round green shield, carved with a broad leaf tree and painted a mossy green. It was apparently given to him by a unicorn in the depths of the Drakkenwood. [[Dimitri Forgehammer|Dimitri]] noted the materials are rare and valuable despite it being ornamental. ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- The party was asked to participate in the Battle of High Hill reenactment, something Ispin always loved acting in (and dying in) ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- The party used knowledge of Ispin's stories to identify the creatures from [[Session 0.5 (4-06)|Session 0.5]] as [[Draconians|draconians]]
