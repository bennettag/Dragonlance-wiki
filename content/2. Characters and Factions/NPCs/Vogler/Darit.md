### Background

A young ward associated with [[Becklin Uth Viharin]], stationed at Thornwall Keep in [[Vogler]]. As a child he was abandoned by his parents in Vogler. Becklin kept a gift for him in her quarters: a custom-fitted set of knightly armor.

### Pre-Story Connections

- Ward and companion of [[Becklin Uth Viharin]]

### In-Story Interactions

- Attended [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s reception at the Brass Crab alongside [[Becklin Uth Viharin|Becklin]]; asked about the Battle of High Hill reenactment but was hushed and asked to leave ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])
- Donned the custom knightly armor Becklin had left for him and joined the defense of [[Vogler]], becoming a knight for a night ([[Session 3 (4-27)|Session 3]])
- Was critically injured in the explosion at the docks during the fall of [[Vogler]]; escaped unconscious but alive ([[Session 4 (5-05)|Session 4]])
- On the water after the evacuation, talked with the party about the future and the possibility of joining the [[Knights of Solamnia]] or an order of high sorcery ([[Session 5 (5-18)|Session 5]])
