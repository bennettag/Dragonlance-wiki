### Background

A wandering halfling merchant who passed through [[Vogler]] regularly for years. Hard to miss, as bright red hair on a halfling his size had a way of announcing him before he rounded the corner. He came from a family with a reputation for magic but showed little sign of it himself, seeming perfectly content to be cheerful, gregarious, and thoroughly ordinary. He always arrived with news from somewhere farther afield and left people in better spirits than he found them.

One visit he came through looking shaken, having heard something on the eastern roads, sounds from creatures he hadn't seen but didn't want to. He didn't linger. A few weeks later he packed up entirely and left, telling anyone who would listen that he was heading off on an adventure of his own. Whether it was genuine wanderlust or fear driving him toward something that felt like a choice, no one knows. Current whereabouts unknown.

### Pre-Story Connections

- [[Davran Raes'Vir]] stayed up late with him once at the Brass Crab, sampling every kind of port the tavern had. In the small hours they talked about magic and its nature, not arriving at any answers but enjoying the contemplation. Davran always wished they'd had more time.
- [[Gregor Fairmount]] remembers him fondly as the kind of person who made the world feel a little larger just by showing up in it. He wonders if they'll cross paths again.
