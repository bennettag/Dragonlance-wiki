### Background

A wizard and old friend of [[Lord Owyn and family|Mira]]'s who visited the Owyn household often throughout [[Asher Owyn|Asher]]'s childhood. He never gave formal lessons, but answered a curious boy's questions and showed him what magic could do for someone who wasn't the biggest or loudest in the room. Those informal teachings were the seed of Asher's spellcasting. His visits have recently stopped without explanation, and with rumors of war growing louder, the silence has started to feel like more than a delayed letter.
