A family of woodworkers in [[Vogler]] with a long-running toy shop and a longer-running rivalry with [[Davran Raes'Vir]]'s shop, The Copper Top.

---
### Arenas Amberthrift (deceased)

The family patriarch. When [[Davran Raes'Vir|Davran]] first came to [[Vogler]], Arenas mounted a smear campaign against him, spreading rumors that his toys were cursed or had negative magical effects on children. The plan backfired and Arenas came to be seen as a paranoid xenophobe.

---
### Ortan Amberthrift

Son of Arenas. A more reasonable man than his father, he competed against [[Davran Raes'Vir|Davran]] on the merits of finely crafted wooden toys, some with moving parts. Davran harbored a grudging respect for that approach.

**In-Story Interactions**
- Was sitting with Horatio at [[Ispin Greenshield|Ispin]]'s reception at the Brass Crab ([[Session 1 (4-13)|Session 1]])

---
### Horatio Amberthrift

Son of Ortan, poised to take over the family shop. He was a bully in his youth, always knowing where the line was and able to wiggle out of trouble with ease. He has practiced no craft of his own, preferring to step on others for money, influence, and power. [[Davran Raes'Vir]] is wary of what lengths he might go to if a lucrative opportunity presented itself.

**Pre-Story Connections**
- Business rival of [[Davran Raes'Vir]]
