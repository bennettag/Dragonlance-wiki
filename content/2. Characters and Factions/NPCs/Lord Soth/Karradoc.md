### Background

A knight bearing Solamnic colors — though not what he appeared to be. His death let loose a possessing spirit bound to him, presumably in service to [[Lord Soth]].

### In-Story Interactions

- Appeared unannounced at Kalaman's War Room in the wake of the council's slaughter, claiming the Knights of Solamnia were en route, before being swiftly cut down by the party. A spectral possessing spirit then fled his body, flying downward — presumably toward [[Lord Soth]] ([[Session 10 (7-27)|Session 10]])
