A group of dockworkers in [[Kalaman]] operating under [[Oflyn Vinhollow]]'s command.

- Identified by [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]] as a group of dockworkers under [[Oflyn Vinhollow]]'s command. Around the same time, a burly dockworker bearing an anchor tattoo on one arm and a red fire-wave on the other was seen collecting a packet from [[Wyhan]]'s Apothecary and carrying it back to the docks ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]])
- The party raided the Fire Fishers' operation at the dockhouse and found them crating [[Red Dragon Army|red dragon]]-marked Boilerdrak components for delivery to a warehouse on Tinker's Row. The workers claimed ignorance, insisting they were "just working" ([[Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
