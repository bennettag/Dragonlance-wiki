- The first, largest, and most powerful of Takhisis’s forces.

- In [[Session 2 (4-20)]] Jeyev recounts that the army had thousands. A red dragon, machines of fire.

### In-Story Operations

- A sivak [[Draconians|draconian]] agent ("Vethrak") killed and replaced council member [[Oflyn Vinhollow]], infiltrating [[Kalaman]]'s leadership for at least four months. Through forged council seals and the [[Fire Fishers]] dockworker network, the agent funneled intelligence on guard patrols and gate schedules out of the city, undermined [[Marshal Nestra Vendri]]'s authority, and redirected her scouts ([[Session 6 (5-25)|Session 6]]–[[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
- Boilerdrak components stamped with the red dragon insignia were being crated at the docks and shipped to a warehouse on Tinker's Row — suggesting the army is assembling siege equipment inside the city ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
- [[Wyhan]] has been developing accelerant fire bombs ("Accelerant Batch 3") for the army. A letter on the sivak's body noted: "She is useful precisely because she believes she is using us" ([[Session 8 (Downtime)|Session 8 Downtime]])
- Intercepted communications reference a "Highlord's column" approaching the valley road, a gate schedule change at River Gate, and coordination with forces inside the city — a plan to open the gates from within during an assault ([[Session 7 (6-09)|Session 7]]–[[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
- A military scroll authorized Agent Vethrak to operate independently with "full Dragon Army authority," signed by a Highlord's clerk ([[Session 8 (Downtime)|Session 8 Downtime]])
- A scroll on the sivak's body read "[[Lord Soth]] is ready," signaling his involvement in the campaign ([[content/4. Notes/Session Notes/Session 8 (6-15)|Session 8]])
