Davran's store is called The Copper Top, small and slender just a block west of the market he lives above the shop. His original claim to fame were metal tops (not actually copper) that with a little enchantment would spin 10x longer than a wooden top. They eventually lost their magic, but were fairly cheap as far as magical toys go. Most of his wares are simple trinket/toys, think old timey gift shop at the science museum with little metal clockwork type sculptures (that, artistically speaking, aren't very impressive). Children took to calling him Mr. Coppertop many years ago and the moniker has somewhat stuck, with even the adults using it as his surname (he rarely corrects them as nobody pronounces Ræs'Vir correctly) For commissions or larger metalworks, he goes to dear Dimitri who is much more talented than himself. Davran has revealed to Dimitri in confidence that he knows without his magic (or elven mystique), he would not be a competent enough crafter to stay in business. One of the reasons he has such a big personality - it keeps the interest in his shop

#### Ally: Missus Morvandra
A matronly spitfire Kender woman who runs the orphanage in Vogler. Davran often volunteers and helps out the less fortunate youth, reading or teaching simple skills, and donating food or coin if having a lucrative month. Morvandra is loving, but can be strict, hoping to impart on those she cares for that kindness is free to give but should not be expected or relied on to survive. I often enjoy the deep levels of gossip we get up to, as it turns out the few orphans tend to overhear quite a good deal of drama within the city

#### Ally: Gustav Braddendorf
an old human very late in his years, we have been close friends since he was in his 20s. I was a close family friend throughout his life, from drinking irresponsibly to double dates, to his wedding, the birth of his children, and even grandchildren. He was the first human I allowed myself to become truly connected to and seeing him near the end of his life fills me with a bittersweet appreciation for our friendship over the years. Cobbler by trade he is since retired and is being cared for by his eldest daughter Hilda.

#### Rival: Horatio Amberthrift
The Amberthrifts are a family of woodworkers who also sell toys in their shop in Vogler. The patriarch Arenas (now deceased), passed on his memories of when I first came to Vogler. Fearing the competition, he mounted a smear campaign against me trying to paint me as a mystyrious creep and danger to the community, then claiming my toys have negative magic effects on children or were cursed. In all his effort, his plans eventually backfired and he became viewed as a paranoid xenophobic man. The business still exists, being ran by his son Ortan, although rumor is he is about to pass it on to his own son Horatio. Ortan was a much more reasonable man, hoping to beat me out by finely crafted wooden toys and some even with moving parts, but Horatio I fear is much more conniving (always a bit of a bully in his youth but one that knew where the line was and could wiggle out of trouble with ease). While distasteful, ive still harbored a respect for the Amberthrifts as crafters. Horatio however has practiced no such craft apart from stepping on others for more money, influence, and power. Who knows what lengths he would go to if he saw a lucrative opportunity.
